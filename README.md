# Takedowns

Documentation of platform bans, deplatformings, and demonetizations affecting lawful firearms-related content creators and brands.

## Firearms creators punished for ordinary gun content (2024–2026)

Ten documented instances ranked by **recency × audience size**. Criterion: punishment for ordinary lawful firearms content (reviews, sport photos, brand pages, range demos)—not manufacturing tutorials, threats, or illegal sales.

| # | Who | Audience | When | Platform | What happened |
|---|-----|----------|------|----------|---------------|
| 1 | **Demolition Ranch** (Matt Carriker) | ~11.8M YouTube subs | Jul 2024 | YouTube | Automatic-weapon videos age-gated 18+ and barred from ads; Carriker reported age-gated videos get ~40% of prior views (~60% loss). Channel monthly views also fell ~18M (2022) → ~8M (later year). [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/gun-youtube-creators-are-leaving-platform-company-cracks-firearm-video-rcna173694) · [Smoking Gun](https://smokinggun.org/popular-guntube-channel-demolition-ranch-to-shut-down/) |
| 2 | **Hickok45** (Greg/John Kinman) | ~7.8M YouTube subs | Jul 2024 | YouTube | Post–June 18 videos removed; ~95% of a 2,700+ video library at risk because lawful gun/accessory sponsor links (spoken or written) became strike/termination territory. [TTAG](https://www.thetruthaboutguns.com/youtube-sets-sights-on-hickok45-videos/) · [The Reload](https://thereload.com/youtube-says-links-to-gun-ammo-dealers-will-trigger-channel-bans/) |
| 3 | **Smith & Wesson** | ~1.6M Facebook followers | Nov 2024 | Facebook/Meta | 15-year page suspended indefinitely over routine product/promo posts (~5 days: Nov 22–27); Meta later called it an “error” and restored it after public backlash. [Fox](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/facebook-silent-after-banning-reinstating-popular-gun-manufacturer) |
| 4 | **Guns.com** | ~395K YouTube subs; 120M+ views | Sep 2024 | YouTube | Multiple strikes and ~1 month of suspensions for content they said matched written policy; quit posting after 13 years and moved to Rumble/X. [Guns.com](https://www.guns.com/news/2024/09/16/why-we-quit-youtube) · [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/gun-youtube-creators-are-leaving-platform-company-cracks-firearm-video-rcna173694) |
| 5 | **The Gun Collective** (Jon Patton) | ~369K YouTube subs | Aug 2024 | YouTube | Dealer-link enforcement (including spoken URLs/ammo sites not clearly listed in writing) treated as channel-killing risk for standard review/sponsorship content. [The Reload](https://thereload.com/youtube-says-links-to-gun-ammo-dealers-will-trigger-channel-bans/) |
| 6 | **GunGuyTV** (Joel Persinger) | ~223K YouTube subs | Fall 2024 | YouTube | Abandoned ~70–80% of prior output (reviews/sponsored firearm content) on YouTube; shifted that work to Rumble. [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/gun-youtube-creators-are-leaving-platform-company-cracks-firearm-video-rcna173694) |
| 7 | **Old Row Outdoors** | ~105K YouTube subs | Fall 2024 | YouTube | Partner Program removal after a policy review of hunting/outdoors firearm content; quit for Rumble. [Reclaim The Net](https://reclaimthenet.org/gun-channels-embrace-rumble-after-youtube-censorship) · [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/gun-youtube-creators-are-leaving-platform-company-cracks-firearm-video-rcna173694) |
| 8 | **Big Horn Armory** | Manufacturer channel (full library wiped) | May 2026 | YouTube | Permanent channel termination after three retroactive strikes on years-old range demos (e.g. shooting frozen chickens)—no build tutorials or sales pitches. [Bearing Arms](https://bearingarms.com/john-petrolino/2026/05/20/prominent-rifle-manufacturer-deplatformed-from-youtube-n1232587) · [TFB](https://www.thefirearmblog.com/blog/big-horn-armory-youtube-channel-is-removed-44828503) |
| 9 | **USA Shooting athletes** (McKenna Geer; also Conner Prince, Vincent Hancock) | Olympic/Paralympic reach; congressional attention | Jul–Aug 2024 | Instagram/Meta | Accounts restricted / non-recommended for competition photos with rifles/shotguns—air-rifle sport content treated as “regulated goods.” [Just the News](https://justthenews.com/nation/culture/instagram-censors-accounts-paralympian-other-athletes-usa-shooting-teams-firearm) · [Rep. Gonzalez](http://gonzalez.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-gonzalez-seeks-answers-meta-over-recent-shadow-bans-team-usas) |
| 10 | **Army West Point Rifle Team** | Official U.S. Military Academy athletics page | 2024 | Facebook/Meta | Page “unpublished” because “Army West Point Rifle goes against our Community Standards.” [WSJ via Bearing Arms](https://bearingarms.com/camedwards/2024/07/25/muzzled-by-meta-competitive-shooters-silenced-on-facebook-instagram-n1225731) · [NSSF](https://www.nssf.org/articles/big-tech-anti-2a-censorship-strikes-again/) |

### Notes on criterion fit (batch 1)

- **Cleanest “punished for guns themselves” fits:** #3 (Meta admitted error), #8 (retroactive strikes on demos), #9–#10 (Olympic/military sport pages).
- **Largest audience hits:** #1–#2.
- **Strongest recent full exits:** #4, #8.

Compiled 2026-09-12 from public reporting; audience figures are approximate as of the cited coverage dates.
