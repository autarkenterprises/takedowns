Firearms creators punished for ordinary gun content
Batch 1 (#1-10): 2024-2026, ranked by recency x audience size.
Batch 2 (#11-25): additional cases, same criterion, ranked within batch.
Criterion: punishment for ordinary lawful firearms content (reviews, sport
photos, brand pages, range demos)--not manufacturing tutorials, threats, or
illegal sales.

1. Demolition Ranch (Matt Carriker)
   Audience: ~11.8M YouTube subs
   When: Jul 2024
   Platform: YouTube
   What: Automatic-weapon videos age-gated 18+ and barred from ads; Carriker
   reported ~60% view loss for that core format of mainstream gun
   entertainment.
   Source: https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/gun-youtube-creators-are-leaving-platform-company-cracks-firearm-video-rcna173694

2. Hickok45 (Greg/John Kinman)
   Audience: ~7.8M YouTube subs
   When: Jul 2024
   Platform: YouTube
   What: Post-June 18 videos removed; ~95% of a 2,700+ video library at risk
   because lawful gun/accessory sponsor links (spoken or written) became
   strike/termination territory.
   Sources: https://www.thetruthaboutguns.com/youtube-sets-sights-on-hickok45-videos/
            https://thereload.com/youtube-says-links-to-gun-ammo-dealers-will-trigger-channel-bans/

3. Smith & Wesson
   Audience: ~1.6M Facebook followers
   When: Nov 2024
   Platform: Facebook/Meta
   What: 15-year page suspended indefinitely over routine product/promo posts;
   Meta later called it an "error" and restored it after public backlash.
   Source: https://www.foxnews.com/politics/facebook-silent-after-banning-reinstating-popular-gun-manufacturer

4. Guns.com
   Audience: ~395K YouTube subs; 120M+ views
   When: Sep 2024
   Platform: YouTube
   What: Multiple strikes and ~1 month of suspensions for content they said
   matched written policy; quit posting after 13 years and moved to Rumble/X.
   Sources: https://www.guns.com/news/2024/09/16/why-we-quit-youtube
            https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/gun-youtube-creators-are-leaving-platform-company-cracks-firearm-video-rcna173694

5. The Gun Collective (Jon Patton)
   Audience: ~369K YouTube subs
   When: Aug 2024
   Platform: YouTube
   What: Dealer-link enforcement (including spoken URLs/ammo sites not clearly
   listed in writing) treated as channel-killing risk for standard
   review/sponsorship content.
   Source: https://thereload.com/youtube-says-links-to-gun-ammo-dealers-will-trigger-channel-bans/

6. GunGuyTV (Joel Persinger)
   Audience: ~223K YouTube subs
   When: Fall 2024
   Platform: YouTube
   What: Abandoned ~70-80% of prior output (reviews/sponsored firearm content)
   on YouTube; shifted that work to Rumble.
   Source: https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/gun-youtube-creators-are-leaving-platform-company-cracks-firearm-video-rcna173694

7. Old Row Outdoors
   Audience: ~105K YouTube subs
   When: Fall 2024
   Platform: YouTube
   What: Partner Program removal after a policy review of hunting/outdoors
   firearm content; quit for Rumble.
   Sources: https://reclaimthenet.org/gun-channels-embrace-rumble-after-youtube-censorship
            https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/gun-youtube-creators-are-leaving-platform-company-cracks-firearm-video-rcna173694

8. Big Horn Armory
   Audience: Manufacturer channel (full library wiped)
   When: May 2026
   Platform: YouTube
   What: Permanent channel termination after three retroactive strikes on
   years-old range demos (e.g. shooting frozen chickens)--no build tutorials
   or sales pitches.
   Sources: https://bearingarms.com/john-petrolino/2026/05/20/prominent-rifle-manufacturer-deplatformed-from-youtube-n1232587
            https://www.thefirearmblog.com/blog/big-horn-armory-youtube-channel-is-removed-44828503

9. USA Shooting athletes (McKenna Geer; also Conner Prince, Vincent Hancock)
   Audience: Olympic/Paralympic reach; congressional attention
   When: Jul-Aug 2024
   Platform: Instagram/Meta
   What: Accounts restricted / non-recommended for competition photos with
   rifles/shotguns--air-rifle sport content treated as "regulated goods."
   Sources: https://justthenews.com/nation/culture/instagram-censors-accounts-paralympian-other-athletes-usa-shooting-teams-firearm
            http://gonzalez.house.gov/media/press-releases/congressman-gonzalez-seeks-answers-meta-over-recent-shadow-bans-team-usas

10. Army West Point Rifle Team
    Audience: Official U.S. Military Academy athletics page
    When: 2024
    Platform: Facebook/Meta
    What: Page "unpublished" because "Army West Point Rifle goes against our
    Community Standards."
    Sources: https://bearingarms.com/camedwards/2024/07/25/muzzled-by-meta-competitive-shooters-silenced-on-facebook-instagram-n1225731
             https://www.nssf.org/articles/big-tech-anti-2a-censorship-strikes-again/

Notes on criterion fit:
- Cleanest "punished for guns themselves" fits: #3 (Meta admitted error), #8
  (retroactive strikes on demos), #9-#10 (Olympic/military sport pages).
- Largest audience hits: #1-#2.
- Strongest recent full exits: #4, #8.

Compiled 2026-09-12 from public reporting; audience figures are approximate as
of the cited coverage dates.
