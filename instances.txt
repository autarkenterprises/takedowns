Firearms creators punished for ordinary gun content
Batch 1 (#1-10): 2024-2026, ranked by recency x audience size.
Batch 2 (#11-25): additional cases, same criterion, ranked within batch.
Batch 3 (#26-35): further cases clearing the same significance bar (only
qualifying cases included).
Criterion: punishment for ordinary lawful firearms content (reviews, sport
photos, brand pages, range demos)--not manufacturing tutorials, threats, or
illegal sales.

1. Demolition Ranch (Matt Carriker)
   Audience: ~11.8M YouTube subs
   When: Jul 2024
   Platform: YouTube
   What: Automatic-weapon videos age-gated 18+ and barred from ads; Carriker
   reported age-gated videos get ~40% of prior views (~60% loss). Channel
   monthly views also fell ~18M (2022) -> ~8M (later year).
   Sources: https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/internet/gun-youtube-creators-are-leaving-platform-company-cracks-firearm-video-rcna173694
